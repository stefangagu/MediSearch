# Problem & Vision

## The Problem

In Romania, choosing a doctor is largely a leap of faith.

- **Information is scattered.** There's no single place to see which doctors practice where, what they specialize in, and which hospitals or private clinics they're affiliated with. Patients piece this together from hospital websites, social media groups, and word of mouth.
- **Trust in doctors is low.** Many patients have had a bad experience, or know someone who has, and have no reliable way to know in advance whether a given doctor is a good fit — both in terms of competence and bedside manner.
- **Reviews exist, but they're fragmented.** Opinions about doctors live across Google reviews, Facebook groups, forums, and private recommendations. There's no central, doctor-specific place where this feedback is collected and easy to weigh.
- **The result:** patients often pick a doctor based on whoever is geographically closest, has an opening soon, or was mentioned once by a friend — not based on a clear picture of who's actually good at what they do.

## Our Vision

MediSearch aims to become **the largest and most trusted doctor database in Romania** — a place where any patient can quickly answer the question *"which doctor should I see, and where?"*

We do this by bringing together, in one place:

- **Who** — the doctor's name and medical specialty
- **Where** — every hospital or private clinic they practice in, with city and location info
- **How good** — ratings and reviews that reflect real patient experiences

## Who We're Building For

- **Primary: Patients in Romania** — anyone trying to find a doctor for themselves or a family member, in their city, for a specific medical need, with some confidence in the choice.
- **Future: Doctors & clinics** — as the platform grows, doctors and clinics benefit from being easy to find and from building a visible reputation based on real patient feedback.

## What Success Looks Like

- A patient can search MediSearch and reliably find doctors relevant to their city and medical need.
- Doctor profiles give a complete picture: specialty, all practice locations, and an honest rating built from real reviews.
- MediSearch becomes the default starting point for Romanians asking "which doctor should I go to?" — replacing guesswork and scattered word-of-mouth with transparent, centralized information.
