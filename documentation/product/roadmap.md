# Roadmap

This outlines the direction MediSearch is heading in, beyond the current sample-data experience described in [Features](features.md).

## 1. Real Doctor Database

Replace the sample dataset with a real, growing database of doctors practicing in Romania — covering as many specialties, cities, hospitals, and private clinics as possible. This is the foundation everything else builds on: search and filters are only as useful as the data behind them.

## 2. Aggregated Trust Score

Today, patient opinions about a doctor are scattered across Google, Facebook groups, forums, and word of mouth. The plan is to **aggregate reviews from multiple public sources into a single, unified rating per doctor**, so patients get one honest score instead of having to piece it together themselves.

## 3. Direct Patient Reviews

Allow patients to leave their own reviews directly on MediSearch, feeding into each doctor's rating alongside aggregated sources — giving the community a direct way to contribute to the platform's trustworthiness.

## 4. Doctor & Clinic Profiles

Let doctors and clinics claim and enrich their own profiles (photos, bios, services offered, schedules), making MediSearch useful for them as a discovery channel, not just for patients.

## 5. Location-Aware Search

Help patients find doctors near them — e.g., "Cardiologie near me" — building on the city and address data already captured in clinic profiles.

## 6. Booking & Contact

Make it easy to go from "I found the right doctor" to "I have an appointment" — starting with clear contact information and clinic links, with appointment requests or booking integrations as a longer-term goal.
